The fine print, in plain words

Privacy

Your bids, your company info, and your logo are stored only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing you type into the calculator is uploaded to us or anyone else — there is no account and no tracking of your numbers. Export a backup file from the app regularly, because clearing your browser data erases what's saved here.

Billing & cancellation

Subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or the Stripe customer portal — no email to us required, no cancellation fee. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the free calculator keeps working either way.
